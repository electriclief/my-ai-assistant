📌 A few notes before you call

  • Ask specifically for "bench testing" or "POST test" — most shops will test a motherboard with a known-good CPU/RAM/PSU and tell you if it posts.
  • Call first, always. Shops take in boards differently: some want the whole PC, some take the board alone, some charge a diagnostic fee ($30–$60 is typical).
  • Thomas TV, ThrifTech and JTG share the same two buildings in Welland — if one is busy, try the other.
  • Emails are best for the shops that list them — attach a photo of the board and the model number (e.g. "ASUS PRIME B450M-A") for a quicker answer.
  • Distances are approximate by road from Wellandport. Everything here is inside your 50 km circle.
