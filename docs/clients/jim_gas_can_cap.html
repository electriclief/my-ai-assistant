🛒 Scepter replacement caps — cheap ~$5–15 CAD

Scepter is a Canadian company (Toronto) and their jerry can parts are widely stocked. The cap itself is a small molded part, so replacement caps are inexpensive — usually around $5–$15 CAD depending on the exact part.

  • Scepter official: scepter.com — parts lookup by model (the 8499 series is the Cabela's 20L military can)
  • Canadian Tire / Princess Auto / Home Depot: carry Scepter cans; the cap can be ordered through the parts counter
  • Cabela's / Bass Pro: where the 8499 JS1710 cans are sold — they stock the spare parts
  • Amazon.ca: search "Scepter jerry can replacement cap"

Prices vary by store and part number — the range above is typical for Scepter small replacement parts. Tell them the numbers on your cap (PAT 87712 / JS1710) and they'll find it instantly.
