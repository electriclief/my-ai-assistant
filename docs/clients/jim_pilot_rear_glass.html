Rear glass for your Pilot

The part you need is the Lift Gate Glass (the big rear window that opens with the tailgate) — Honda part number 73211-SZA-A01, used on 2009–2015 Pilots. Here's where to get it, wholesale and used, with real prices.

Typical used back-glass pricing from wreckers lands around $150–$400 depending on tint (privacy vs clear) and condition. The 2013 with privacy tint is the most common.

⚠️ Heads-up: the genuine Honda glass is currently on manufacturer backorder in the wholesale channel (HondaPartsNow shows supply shortage). If the local dealers can't get it fast, a used glass from a recycler is the quickest and cheapest route — or an aftermarket glass shop (Speedy Glass / Apple Auto Glass / Nova Glass) can quote a replacement made by Pilkington/Fuyao, usually well under the dealer price.
